Business guide

Where the money is, and what to do about it

AI is a mass-market, capex-saturated reality. Quantum is early-commercial. The combined story is mostly hybrid AI + HPC + quantum workflows and quantum-safe security. Every figure below is labelled by source, year and whether it is a forecast or a reported fact.

Read the labels. Forecast means an analyst projection, not an outcome. Fact means reported or published. Vendor claim comes from the vendor's or partner's own release.

Investment and capex trends

AI capex Guidance

  • Amazon, Alphabet, Microsoft and Meta guided roughly $720–745B combined for 2026 (trade press on July 2026 earnings). Five hyperscalers incl. Oracle: ~$780B (Vanguard-compiled).
  • Moody's: $785B in 2026, approaching $1T in 2027. Citi: $5.35T cumulative for four hyperscalers through 2030 Forecast.
  • Gartner (Oct 2026): ~$7.5T cumulative AI capex 2025–2030, about $2T/yr by 2030 Forecast. Bain (Sep 2025): ~$2T of annual new revenue needed to fund compute scaling by 2030.
  • Meta guided $135–145B for 2026; Q3-2026 capex of $34.9B and its first negative free-cash-flow quarter in years.

Bubble indicators Forecast

  • Gartner (Oct 2026): data-centre owners need $8.5T cumulative revenue by 2030 for a 12% return, but are likely to see no more than $3.6T.
  • Over $3.5T of AI infrastructure spend could be exposed to impairment by end-2030.
  • Neocloud economics are weaker than those of diversified hyperscalers.
  • Frontier labs: Anthropic $65B Series H at $965B post-money (28 May 2026, CNBC); OpenAI reported valuation $852B and ~10 GW secured capacity. Run-rate revenue figures are unaudited and company-sourced.

Quantum capital Fact

  • Record $3.9B VC in 2025; accelerating in 2026 (over $4B reported by late Sep 2026). Venture-growth share of deal value rose from ~1% to 27.5%.
  • IonQ completed a ~$2B equity offering; IQM heading to Nasdaq via SPAC; DARPA QBI Stage C awards (IonQ up to $300M).
  • McKinsey sample of 162 large companies: one-third spent over $10M on QC in 2025, 7% over $50M.
  • Vendor revenue is thin against valuations; one independent brief found no QC company yet earning revenue from useful computation itself (mostly R&D contracts, access, hardware).

Where AI and quantum capital meet Secondary report

  • Nvidia and BlackRock were reported among the largest quantum investors in 2025 (about $1.6B and $1.7B, PitchBook data via a secondary report).
  • US companion quantum-innovation executive order signed 22 Jun 2026; EU, Canada, Australia and India are setting 2028–2031 PQC clocks.

AI Today

Where AI and quantum genuinely intersect

Hybrid HPC + AI + QPU 3–5y

NVIDIA CUDA-Q/NVQLink-style integration; AstraZeneca–IonQ–AWS–NVIDIA. McKinsey says winners invest in HPC–QC integration.

AI for quantum Today

ML for error-correction decoding, calibration and circuit compilation (Google, NVIDIA, IBM). Where AI most plausibly speeds up quantum timelines.

Quantum for AI Speculative

No demonstrated advantage on commercial ML tasks. Treat 'quantum AI' marketing sceptically. No consensus evidence that AGI needs quantum hardware.

Business risks

Hype & valuation

Revenue vs valuation mismatch in both fields; Gartner puts GenAI in the Trough of Disillusionment (2026).

Execution

Pilots without workflow redesign (MIT, McKinsey).

Lock-in & concentration

Gartner: assume a more concentrated AI supply base by 2030; no dominant quantum modality yet.

Cryptographic

Harvest-now-decrypt-later for long-lived data; migration takes years.

Benchmark

Quantum advantage claims are narrow and contested; classical algorithms improve, so re-test baselines.

Supply

Power, memory and chip constraints for AI; memory pricing pressure (Gartner).

Regulatory & geopolitical

Export controls, EU AI Act, sovereign AI, PQC mandates, local opposition to data centres.

AGI/ASI scenario

Rapid capability jumps could reshape labour and competition. Keep scenario plans, not point forecasts.

Talent

Quantum: demand outruns supply

McKinsey data cited by Forbes: roughly one qualified candidate per three open roles; universities graduate only a few thousand quantum-trained people a year (estimate from secondary sources). Dedicated full-time quantum roles have replaced part-time ones. Strategy: small hybrid teams (domain scientist + algorithm developer + HPC/cloud engineer), QCaaS, university partnerships.

AI: from model-building to integration

The scarce skills are workflow integration, evaluation, data engineering and governance.

What to do now: action playbook

PQC migration is the highest-urgency track; it is backed by federal deadlines and does not depend on quantum hardware arriving early.

1. Post-quantum cryptography Highest urgency

  1. Inventory cryptography (CBOM) across apps, devices, vendors and certificates.
  2. Prioritise by data shelf-life (harvest-now-decrypt-later): long-lived confidential data first; key establishment (ML-KEM) before signatures (ML-DSA).
  3. Write it into contracts: require vendor PQC delivery dates; check FIPS 140-3 validation.
  4. Align to the earliest applicable deadline: federal high-value assets and contractors 2030–31; other civilian systems 2035; EU/France regulators earlier.
  5. Build crypto-agility (hybrid classical + PQC modes) so algorithms can be swapped.

2. Quantum readiness Start small

  1. Appoint an owner and a 12-month plan. Map exposure: optimisation-heavy, simulation-heavy or crypto-heavy. Split strategy into what can wait vs what must start now (Gartner frame).
  2. Run 1–3 scoped pilots on QCaaS/hybrid solvers with an explicit classical baseline and kill criteria. Co-development is becoming the standard model (McKinsey).
  3. Start small. One-third of large companies analysed spent over $10M in 2025; most don't need that. A small team plus cloud credits is enough to begin.
  4. Build HPC–QC integration skills and keep algorithm IP in-house.
  5. Watch fault-tolerance milestones, not qubit counts: IBM 2029, Quantinuum Sol 2027 / Apollo 2029, Google.

3. AI adoption Now

  1. Tie each use case to a P&L metric and redesign the workflow, not just add a copilot.
  2. Embedded AI first for quick wins; custom/agentic systems only where you hold a data advantage.
  3. Governance and cost control: usage tracking, model portability, data sovereignty.
  4. Scenario-plan for much more capable AI (staffing, security, partner concentration) without committing capital to speculative timelines.
  5. Don't be the bag-holder of AI infrastructure: rent capacity and keep multi-vendor options; impairment risk sits with providers, but price shocks and discontinuations reach customers.
The deadlines. US Executive Order 'Securing the Nation Against Advanced Cryptographic Attacks' (signed 22 Jun 2026; 91 FR 38483) requires federal high-value assets to use PQC for key establishment by 31 Dec 2030 and signatures by 31 Dec 2031, with a FAR rule for contractors proposed by 19 Dec 2026. Other civilian systems: 2035 (OMB M-26-15). NIST IR 8547's 2030/2035 dates are still draft. Sources disagree on the order's number, so cite it by title and date. Gartner: fewer than half of CISOs have started PQC, and only 24% of those show measurable progress. No crypto-relevant quantum computer exists today; Gidney (Google, 2025) estimated RSA-2048 needs under 1M noisy qubits.
