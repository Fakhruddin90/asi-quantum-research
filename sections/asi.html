01 · Superintelligence

Artificial superintelligence: hypothetical, but being actively forecast

Where the field stands as of October 2026: what ANI, AGI and ASI actually mean, who is claiming what and when, the physical limits of scaling, and how regulators are responding. Each claim is tagged as fact, claim or speculation.

From narrow AI to superintelligence

ANI

Narrow AI

Systems that perform specific tasks: chess, translation, protein folding. Everything deployed historically.

Fact
AGI

Artificial General Intelligence

Human-level cognition across most tasks. There is no agreed test and definitions differ by lab; Google DeepMind has published a levels-of-AGI framework (breadth × depth × autonomy). Not achieved by consensus. The International AI Safety Report 2026 calls frontier capability "jagged": strong at expert-level maths and code, weak at some simple tasks.

Not achieved
ASI

Artificial Superintelligence

Bostrom (2014): "any intellect that greatly exceeds the cognitive performance of humans in virtually all domains of interest." It does not exist.

Speculative

Forecasters use operational definitions too: AI Impacts' "high-level machine intelligence" (unaided machines outperform humans at every task, more cheaply); AI Futures Project's "Automated Coder" → superhuman coder → ASI milestones. OpenAI describes itself as "a superintelligence research company".

Bostrom's three forms of superintelligence

⚡

Speed

A human-like mind running much faster.

🧩

Collective

Many minds aggregated into a larger whole.

🧠

Quality

A mind that is qualitatively smarter, not just faster or more numerous.

Amodei's "country of geniuses in a datacenter" is essentially a speed + collective scenario in Bostrom's terms.

Can scaling continue to 2030?

Epoch finds ~2e29 FLOP training runs by 2030 feasible (GPT-4 ≈ 2e25 FLOP). Binding constraints: power first, then chip production. More than 100 GW of AI capacity worldwide (>50 GW in the US, ~5% of US generation capacity) is plausible by 2030. Whether spending continues is speculation.

Epoch AI; Epoch/EPRI · Extrapolation

Reasoning models & agents

  • Inference-time scaling drove "particularly large" gains in maths, software engineering and science over the past year (International AI Safety Report 2026).
  • OpenAI reports gold-medal-level performance at the IMO and IOI (2025). This is OpenAI's own evaluation, not an IMO-coordinated certification at the time.
  • METR time horizon (task length an AI completes at 50% reliability): doubling roughly every ~7 months over ~6 years, ~4–5 months since 2024. METR's Time Horizon 1.1 (29 Jan 2026) measured o3 at ≈ 2 hours (116–119 min).
  • Agents still struggle to recover from basic errors in long workflows.

Infrastructure build-out

OpenAI's "Stargate" is a multi-site build-out, for example a 1 GW campus in Michigan (Oracle / Related Digital) and an SB Energy partnership (9 Jan 2026). The International AI Safety Report notes developers have announced hundreds of billions of dollars of data-centre investment.

Fact · progress through 2030 remains uncertain; trends are consistent with continued improvement.

The central technical disagreement. Altman and Amodei hold that the current trajectory suffices. Sutskever argues pure scaling is hitting limits and that new ideas (better generalization, sample efficiency) are needed. This is unresolved. Energy and chips are the physical bottleneck, which is the natural bridge to the quantum-computing sections: whether energy-efficient or fundamentally different compute can help is open and speculative.

Main technical routes to ASI

1 · Scaled pre-training + RL/reasoning

Transformer LLMs with inference-time compute. The dominant strategy at OpenAI, Anthropic and Google DeepMind.

2 · Agentic systems / AI R&D automation

AI doing AI research, the "intelligence explosion" route. AI Futures models it as coding time-horizon → Automated Coder → superhuman researcher.

3 · New learning paradigms

Better generalization, continual learning, human-like sample efficiency (the SSI "age of research" thesis).

4 · Embodied & scientific AI

Altman expects robots in 2027; OpenAI's "FrontierScience" evaluates research tasks.

5 · Brain-inspired, hybrid, quantum-accelerated

Speculative Covered in the quantum and relationship sections.

Risks and alignment

Three risk families

The International AI Safety Report 2026 groups risks into misuse, malfunctions and systemic disruption. It recommends "defence-in-depth" (layered safeguards), notes open-weight models cannot be recalled and have easily removed safeguards, and urges societal resilience measures.

Fact

Core ASI arguments

Bostrom's control problem and Hinton's point that we have "never created beings smarter than us": loss of control, power concentration, bio and cyber misuse. Hinton warns AI could design viruses or cyberattacks and cause "chaos just by talking to people".

Speculative / claim

Amodei's categories

  • Autonomous AI takeover
  • Misuse by bad actors (e.g. terrorists with "mercenary" AI)
  • Economic disruption and mass unemployment
  • Indirect destabilization
Claim

Alignment research directions

Scalable oversight, interpretability, evaluations (METR, UK AISI), red-teaming, control and monitoring. These are described only generally in the Safety Report; specific lab methods were not individually verified.

Calls for a prohibition

The Future of Life Institute's "Statement on Superintelligence" (22 Oct 2025) calls for prohibiting superintelligence development until there is broad scientific consensus on safety and strong public buy-in. About 65,000 signatories joined within days.

Fact

Insider dissent

One report (Sep 2026) says a young researcher resigned from Anthropic, alleging OpenAI and Anthropic are "racing straight to self-improving superintelligence". Single report; treat with caution.

Governance and regulation

🇪🇺 EU AI Act

Regulation 2024/1689. Prohibited practices (Feb 2025) and general-purpose AI model rules (Aug 2025) apply. Commission enforcement powers over GPAI and Art. 50 transparency obligations apply from 2 Aug 2026.

The Digital Omnibus on AI (Reg. 2026/1744, in force 27 Jul 2026) delayed high-risk obligations to 2 Dec 2027 (Annex III) and 2 Aug 2028 (Annex I). Art. 50 breaches carry fines up to €15M / 3%.

🇺🇸 US federal

EO 14179 (23 Jan 2025) removed the Biden-era AI order. EO 14365 (11 Dec 2025, "Ensuring a National Policy Framework for AI") sets up a DOJ AI Litigation Task Force to challenge state AI laws and conditions some BEAD funds. It cannot itself invalidate state laws.

California SB 53

Transparency in Frontier AI Act, signed 29 Sep 2025: the first US state law expressly regulating frontier developers. Disclosure / safety-framework and incident-reporting design; successor to the vetoed SB 1047. The legal challenge and preemption fight is ongoing and unresolved.

International

The International AI Safety Report (Bengio-led, UK-hosted, 2nd edition Feb 2026; 100+ experts, 30+ countries) informs policy but is not binding. As far as this research found, no binding international regime on superintelligence exists.

Civil society

The FLI prohibition statement (Oct 2025) is the most visible call to stop superintelligence development outright.

Direction of travel

Governance is fragmenting: the EU is delaying high-risk rules, the US federal government is pushing preemption against state frontier-AI laws, and some groups want outright prohibition.

Not covered: Chinese governance, UN processes, and GPAI-model fine levels under the EU Act (not verified).

What we know vs. what we speculate

Known

  • ASI does not exist; AGI is not achieved by consensus.
  • Frontier training compute grows ~4–5×/yr; data-centre electricity is projected to pass 1,000 TWh by 2030 (IEA).
  • Reasoning models have made real gains; capability stays jagged.
  • Regulation exists (EU AI Act, California SB 53) but is unsettled and fragmenting.

Speculative or contested

  • When, or whether, AGI and ASI arrive. Forecasts range from 2027 to 2047+.
  • Whether scaling alone suffices, or new research paradigms are needed.
  • Extinction probabilities. They are subjective and even Hinton says nobody knows.
  • Whether spending, power and chips keep up to 2030.
  • Any role for quantum computing in reaching ASI.

Several 2026 items rest on secondary outlets (Hassabis WSJ interview, Metaculus median, Hinton/Newsnight via Anadolu). Verify against primary sources before relying on them.
