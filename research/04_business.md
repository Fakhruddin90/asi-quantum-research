# 04 — Business Use Cases & Markets: AI → AGI/Superintelligence × Quantum Computing

*Research date: 25 Oct 2026. Labelling convention: **[Fact]** = reported/published; **[Vendor claim]** = from the vendor's or partner's own press release; **[Forecast]** = analyst projection, not an outcome; **[Est.]** = my own judgment. Figures are reproduced from the cited sources; where sources conflict I say so.*

---

## 1. Executive summary

- **AI (A) is a mass-market, capex-saturated reality; AGI/superintelligence is a strategic bet, not a product.** Gartner forecasts worldwide AI spending of **$2.67T in 2026** (+49.5%) and **$3.64T in 2027** [Forecast, Gartner, Sep 2026]. The four largest hyperscalers guided roughly **$720–745B of combined 2026 capex** [Fact/guidance, trade press summarising July 2026 earnings]. Yet enterprise returns are uneven: McKinsey's 2025 survey found only **39% report any enterprise-level EBIT impact**, and nearly two-thirds had not begun scaling AI across the enterprise.
- **Quantum (B) is early-commercial, not yet "useful at scale."** McKinsey (Quantum Technology Monitor 2026, Apr 2026) puts QC company revenue at **>$1B in 2025**, forecast **$3.2–4.4B by 2028**, and "value at stake" for industry of **$1.3–2.7T by 2035** [Forecast]. No peer-reviewed, commercially priced, *general* quantum advantage on a business workload exists yet; the best evidence is hybrid pilots (HSBC, Ford Otosan, JPMorgan, BMW, AstraZeneca).
- **The combined (C) story is mostly "AI + HPC + QPU" hybrid workflows and quantum-safe security,** not "a superintelligence running on a quantum computer." The nearest-term, highest-certainty business action is **post-quantum cryptography (PQC) migration**, now backed by US federal deadlines (2030/2031) and a Gartner warning that conventional asymmetric crypto becomes unsafe by 2030.
- **Both sectors carry bubble/valuation risk.** Gartner (Oct 2026) estimates AI data-centre owners need **$8.5T cumulative revenue by 2030** to earn a 12% return but are likely to see no more than **$3.6T**; **>$3.5T** of AI infrastructure spend could be exposed to impairment by end-2030 [Forecast].

## 3. Maturity key & state of the technology

- **AI today:** deployed at scale in software engineering, customer service, document work, coding, search; agents spreading (Box SAI26 survey: 83% of orgs run AI agents, 19% at autonomous scale — vendor survey). 
- **AGI/ASI:** Frontier labs state AGI-level goals; no consensus on timing. Business planning should treat it as **scenario risk** rather than a dated deliverable.
- **Quantum hardware milestones:** Google Willow "Quantum Echoes" (Oct 2025) — first *verifiable* advantage claim on a physics task (OTOCs; ~13,000× vs best classical) [Google Research]. Quantinuum Helios (Nov 2025; 98 qubits, 99.921% 2-qubit fidelity; 48 error-corrected logical qubits claimed). IBM targets fault-tolerant "Starling" by 2029. IonQ targets 2M physical qubits by 2030 (roadmap). Gartner: production-grade fault-tolerant systems still **5–10 years out**.
- **Cryptanalytic threat:** Gidney (Google, May 2025) estimated RSA-2048 breakable with <1M noisy qubits in <1 week; later 2026 papers claim <100k physical qubits on paper. No such machine exists. Global Risk Institute's 2025 expert survey: 28–49% see it "quite possible" within ~10 years.

## 4. Industry-by-industry

Each row: **(A) AI**, **(B) Quantum**, **(C) Combined**, with maturity **Today / 3–5y / 10y+** *[Est. mine unless sourced]* and named examples.

### 4.1 Pharma / drug discovery
- **A – AI:** *Today (clinical proof emerging).* Insilico Medicine's generative-AI-discovered TNIK inhibitor **rentosertib** (IPF) published a randomized Phase IIa in *Nature Medicine* (2025) and **Phase III initiation was announced 7 Jul 2026** [company PR]. Isomorphic Labs raised $2.1B (May 2026); secondary sources conflict on whether it has entered Phase 1 or 2 — verify before citing clinical stage. Boehringer Ingelheim–Microsoft AI clinical-development partnership (22 Oct 2026).
- **B – Quantum:** *Today: pilots; 3–5y: hybrid molecular simulation in R&D workflows; 10y+: fault-tolerant chemistry accuracy.* AstraZeneca–IonQ–AWS–NVIDIA hybrid QC-classical workflow (2025, vendor-announced); Moderna–IBM simulated secondary structure of a 60-nucleotide mRNA on a quantum computer (longest folded to date; research demo); Boehringer Ingelheim has worked with Google Quantum AI and PsiQuantum on cytochrome P450. McKinsey sizes pharma QC value at **up to ~$400B by 2035 (≈2–12% of sales)** [Forecast].
- **C – Combined:** quantum-generated training data/simulations feeding ML property predictors; AI proposes candidates, QC refines binding energetics. *Mostly 5–10y.*
- **ROI evidence:** Real but not yet quantified in $ for QC. AI: faster candidate-to-clinic timelines are claimed by AI biotechs; efficacy outcomes remain the key unknown.

### 4.2 Finance (portfolio optimisation, risk, fraud)
- **A:** *Today.* Fraud/AML, credit scoring, coding assistants, research agents are mainstream. Gartner expects AI cybersecurity spending to roughly double in 2026 ($51B).
- **B:** *Today: pilots; 3–5y: optimisation/pricing/Monte-Carlo speedups; 10y+: broad risk modelling.* 
  - **HSBC + IBM (25 Sep 2025):** quantum-classical approach improved prediction of fill probability in European corporate bond RFQs by **up to 34%** vs. classical baseline — *[Vendor claim; IBM Heron; "promising trial," not a production P&L; critics note classical baselines and no cost/latency comparison disclosed]*.
  - **JPMorgan + Quantinuum (Nature, Mar 2025):** certified randomness on a 56-qubit H2 machine — a verifiable beyond-classical task; relevance to finance is cryptography/fairness, not yet trading P&L.
  - McKinsey sizes finance QC value at **$400–600B by 2035 (≈3–4.5% avg.)**, with biggest percentages in asset/wealth management and risk/cyber.
- **C:** AI models for signal generation plus quantum-sampling or optimisation subroutines; PQC for payment/crypto infrastructure (the most time-critical piece).
- **ROI:** Isolated lab-grade improvements (HSBC 34% on a *prediction metric*); no audited production savings.

### 4.3 Logistics / supply chain / transport
- **A:** *Today.* Demand forecasting, routing, warehouse robotics, agentic procurement.
- **B:** *Today: annealing/hybrid solvers in niche production; 3–5y: scaled routing/scheduling pilots; 10y+.* Einride–IonQ collaboration (2025); McKinsey sizes travel/transport/logistics at **$200–500B by 2035**; Volkswagen's Lisbon bus-routing D-Wave demo (2019) remains a canonical (small) pilot.
- **C:** AI forecasts feed QUBO/hybrid solvers for re-optimisation; "quantum digital twins" are being embedded in pilot TMS systems (McKinsey 2026).
- **ROI:** McKinsey impact hypotheses of ~1–5% of logistics COGS by 2035 [Forecast]; no published audited ROI for quantum.

### 4.4 Energy, grid & batteries
- **A:** *Today.* Grid forecasting, predictive maintenance, trading; AI-driven materials discovery (e.g., DeepMind's GNoME predicted ~2.2M crystal structures, ~380k stable — computational predictions, not commercial batteries yet).
- **B:** *3–5y: grid/dispatch optimisation pilots; 10y+: battery/catalyst chemistry.* E.ON named by McKinsey as an active collaborator; Aramco deployed a Pasqal QC (first dedicated industrial QC in the Middle East, 2025); BMW–Quantinuum (expanded May 2026) targets oxygen-reduction catalysis at platinum for fuel cells.
- **C:** AI-screened candidates + quantum-accurate energetics for electrolytes/catalysts. *10y+ for impact.*
- **Business note:** AI's own power demand (data centres ~46% of US private office construction value in 2025 per BOMA via Fortune) is itself a major energy-sector demand driver.

### 4.5 Materials & chemicals
- **A:** *Today/3–5y.* ML interatomic potentials, autonomous labs, generative design.
- **B:** *3–5y: hybrid pilots; 10y+: a priori design.* **BASF–D-Wave** completed a proof-of-concept on manufacturing-workflow efficiency (Nov 2025); **Mitsubishi Chemical–IBM** research on quantum simulation of materials (OLED-type chemistry, research stage); BMW–Quantinuum catalysis. McKinsey sizes chemicals QC value at **$450–800B by 2035 (5–9% of sales)**, with R&D virtualisation potentially saving 20–50% of R&D spend [Forecast, upper-end optimistic].
- **C:** AI+QC "virtual R&D" is the most-cited long-run synergy.

### 4.6 Cybersecurity
- **A:** *Today.* AI is both defender tool and attacker tool; Gartner forecasts AI cybersecurity spend $51B (2026) → $86B (2027).
- **B:** Quantum is primarily a **threat** (Shor's algorithm) with defensive products (PQC, QKD, QRNG). *Today: PQC migration mandatory planning; 3–5y: 2030–2031 regulatory deadlines; 10y+: crypto-relevant QC possible.*
- **Policy (Fact):** US **Executive Order "Securing the Nation Against Advanced Cryptographic Attacks"** (signed 22 Jun 2026; initially reported as EO 14409, later sources say EO 14412 — check the Federal Register, 91 FR 38483) requires federal high-value assets to use PQC for key establishment by **31 Dec 2030** and signatures by **31 Dec 2031**, with a FAR rule for contractors proposed by 19 Dec 2026. Remaining civilian systems: 2035 (OMB M-26-15). NIST IR 8547's 2030/2035 dates are still draft. Google and Cloudflare set 2029 internal PQC targets (per postquantum.com). Gartner: <half of CISOs have started PQC and only 24% of those show measurable progress.
- **C:** AI-assisted cryptographic inventory/CBOM discovery; AI accelerating vulnerability research raises the stakes for "crypto-agility."

### 4.7 Manufacturing
- **A:** *Today.* Predictive maintenance, vision QC, scheduling, industrial copilots, humanoid/robotics pilots.
- **B:** **Ford Otosan (Ford/Koç JV, Türkiye)** — deployed a D-Wave hybrid-quantum production-sequencing application for Ford Transit (more than 1,500 variants) in production; reduced scheduling time for 1,000 vehicles from **30 min to <5 min** [Vendor-co-announced, 31 Mar 2025]. Caveat: speed vs. their prior method, not proof of quantum advantage; hybrid solver includes classical components.
- **Maturity:** Today (niche), 3–5y (more lines/plants), 10y+ (simulation-driven design).

### 4.8 Telecom
- **A:** *Today.* Network automation, churn, AI-RAN, agentic care.
- **B:** *Today: QKD/quantum-secure networking pilots; 3–5y: network optimisation; 10y+: quantum networks.* Quantum Computing Inc. launched a quantum-secure networking platform for telecom/data-centre operators (ECOC 2025); IonQ/Capella space QKD plans. McKinsey sizes telecom QC impact in the lower tier (**$50–150B** category band with media/tech) [Forecast]. PQC migration of core/SIM/5G signalling is a major telecom workload.
- **C:** AI-driven spectrum/network planning with quantum optimisers (research).

### 4.9 Automotive
- **A:** *Today.* ADAS/autonomy stacks, generative design, supply-chain AI.
- **B:** BMW (Quantinuum multi-year, May 2026; Classiq+NVIDIA on architecture optimisation; Airbus–BMW "Quantum Challenge" has grown into a Global Quantum+AI Challenge 2026), Ford Otosan (above), Volkswagen (traffic/battery research with D-Wave/Google), Hyundai noted by McKinsey as a top-ranked Asian quantum-ready firm. Europe (esp. Germany) leads in industry quantum readiness (McKinsey 2026).
- **Maturity:** Today (optimisation pilots), 3–5y (materials/battery), 10y+ (full simulation).

### 4.10 Aerospace & defence
- **A:** *Today.* Design-space exploration, maintenance, ISR analytics.
- **B:** Airbus (quantum challenge problems, e.g., wing-box loading/cargo; partnership with BMW and Quantinuum noted May 2026); quantum **sensing** (Honeywell US-government GPS-denied navigation contracts, 2025) is arguably nearer to deployment than quantum computing.
- **Maturity:** Sensing today–3y; computing 5–10y+.

### 4.11 Climate
- **A:** *Today.* AI weather/climate emulation, grid and emissions monitoring.
- **B:** *10y+.* Better catalysts for carbon capture, fertiliser (nitrogen fixation), batteries; quantum sensing for monitoring. 
- **C:** AI downscaling + quantum-accurate chemistry. Trade-off: AI's energy demand vs. its climate-optimisation promise.

## 5. Combined (C): where AI and quantum genuinely intersect

1. **Hybrid HPC + AI + QPU workflows** (NVIDIA CUDA-Q/NVQLink-style integration; AstraZeneca–IonQ–AWS–NVIDIA). McKinsey 2026 highlights "hybrid computing and integration in data centers" as a deep-dive theme and says winners invest in HPC–QC integration.
2. **AI for quantum:** ML for error-correction decoding, calibration, circuit compilation (Google, NVIDIA, IBM work). This is where AI most plausibly accelerates quantum timelines.
3. **Quantum for AI:** speculative (quantum ML, sampling). No demonstrated advantage on commercial ML tasks; treat vendor "quantum AI" marketing sceptically.
4. **Security convergence:** AI accelerates both attack and defence; PQC is the common denominator.
5. **Capital convergence:** Nvidia and BlackRock were reported among the largest quantum investors in 2025 (≈$1.6B and ≈$1.7B respectively, secondary report of PitchBook data) — mainstream AI-era capital is flowing into quantum.
6. **"Superintelligence needs quantum?"** No consensus evidence that AGI requires quantum hardware; classical GPU scaling is the dominant path. [Est.]

## 8. Business risks

1. **Hype/valuation risk** (both AI and quantum); revenue vs. valuation mismatch; Gartner GenAI "trough."
2. **Execution risk:** pilots without workflow redesign (MIT/McKinsey).
3. **Vendor lock-in / concentration:** Gartner advises designing AI products assuming a *more concentrated* supply base by 2030; no dominant quantum modality yet (McKinsey).
4. **Cryptographic risk:** harvest-now-decrypt-later for long-lived data; migration is multi-year.
5. **Benchmark risk:** quantum "advantage" claims are narrow and contested; classical algorithms improve (re-test baselines).
6. **Talent scarcity:** McKinsey data cited by Forbes/Firgun: ~1 qualified quantum candidate for every 3 open roles; universities graduate only a few thousand quantum-trained people per year (estimate in secondary sources).
7. **Power, memory and chip supply** constraints for AI; Gartner notes memory pricing pressure.
8. **Regulatory/geopolitical:** export controls, EU AI Act, sovereign AI, PQC mandates, data-centre local opposition.
9. **AGI/ASI-scenario risk:** rapid capability jumps could reshape labour and competitive dynamics; keep scenario plans, not point forecasts.

## 9. Talent

- Quantum: demand outpaces supply; McKinsey notes dedicated full-time quantum roles (quantum engineers, algorithm developers) replaced part-time roles over the past decade; typical large-company budgets now >$5M for hundreds of players. Strategy: build small hybrid teams (domain scientist + algorithm developer + HPC/cloud engineer), use QCaaS, and partner with universities.
- AI: shift from model-building to workflow integration, evaluation, data engineering, and governance.

## 10. What a business should do now

### Quantum readiness
1. **Appoint an owner and a 12-month plan;** map exposure (optimisation-heavy, simulation-heavy, or crypto-heavy). Gartner's frame: split strategy into "what can wait vs. what must start now."
2. **Run 1–3 well-scoped pilots** on QCaaS/hybrid solvers with an explicit classical baseline and kill criteria (McKinsey: co-development is becoming the standard engagement model).
3. **Budget scale:** one-third of large companies analysed spent >$10M in 2025 — most do not need that; start at a small team + cloud credits.
4. **Build HPC–QC integration skills** and hold algorithm IP.
5. **Monitor fault-tolerance milestones** (IBM 2029, Quantinuum Sol 2027/Apollo 2029, Google) rather than qubit counts.

### PQC migration (highest urgency)
1. **Cryptographic inventory / CBOM** across apps, devices, vendors, certificates.
2. **Prioritise by data shelf-life** (HNDL): long-lived confidential data first; key establishment (ML-KEM) before signatures (ML-DSA).
3. **Contract language:** require vendors to commit to PQC delivery dates; check FIPS 140-3 validation.
4. **Align to the earliest applicable deadline:** federal HVA/contractors 2030–31; others 2035 (NIST draft); regulators in EU/France etc. earlier.
5. **Crypto-agility** (hybrid classical+PQC modes) so algorithms can be swapped.

### AI adoption
1. **Tie each use case to a P&L metric;** redesign the workflow, not just add a copilot.
2. **Prefer embedded AI in existing platforms for quick wins,** custom/agentic systems where data advantage exists (Gartner trend).
3. **Governance & cost control** (usage tracking, model portability, data sovereignty).
4. **Scenario-plan for much more capable AI** (AGI-level) — staffing, security, partner concentration — without committing capital to speculative timelines.
5. **Avoid being the bag-holder of AI infrastructure:** rent capacity, keep multi-vendor options (impairment risk is on providers, but price shocks/discontinuations reach customers).
